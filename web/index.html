Method and limitations

Formula: 2 × sodium + glucose ÷ 18 + BUN ÷ 2.8.

Estimated tonicity excludes urea. This model excludes ethanol and other unmeasured osmoles. It is an approximation of measured osmolality; it cannot identify the cause of an abnormal result. Use matched laboratory samples and locally validated reference intervals. Clinical decisions require independent assessment.
